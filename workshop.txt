title=[42.22.2] Repeat Last Dish
description=[h1]Repeat Last Dish[/h1]
description=Remembers the ingredients you put into your last dish, in order, and makes it again from a right-click on an empty bowl or pan.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Make a salad, stir fry, soup... the usual way. The base item and every ingredient, in order, are recorded.
description=[*]Right-click an empty base of the same kind: [b]Repeat last dish[/b]. Select several to make several dishes in a row.
description=[*]Ingredients come from your inventory and the containers next to you (fridge, counters, floor). Fresh or stale doesn't matter, older first. [b]Rotten food is never used.[/b]
description=[*]Normal add-ingredient actions and times; leftovers go back where they came from.
description=[*]Greyed out with a list of what's missing when an ingredient isn't there.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=Client-side Lua using vanilla actions only. Tested in single player. Multiplayer: 0.1.1 fixes dishes made on a server not being recorded (the option never appeared); tested with the server's timing simulated, not yet on a real server. Safe to add to or remove from a save.
description=
description=[h2]한국어[/h2]
description=마지막으로 만든 요리(샐러드, 볶음 등)의 재료를 넣은 순서대로 기억했다가, 같은 빈 그릇·프라이팬을 우클릭하면 [b]마지막 요리 반복[/b]으로 다시 만듭니다. 여러 개를 선택하면 차례로 만듭니다. 재료는 인벤토리와 주변 용기(냉장고 등)에서 꺼내며, 신선도는 상관없이(오래된 것부터) 쓰고 상한 음식은 쓰지 않습니다. 싱글에서 테스트했습니다. 멀티: 0.1.1에서 서버에서 만든 요리가 기록되지 않아 메뉴가 안 뜨던 문제를 고쳤습니다(서버 타이밍을 흉내 낸 테스트로 확인, 실제 서버 확인은 아직).
