title=[42.22.2] Repeat Last Dish
description=[h1]Repeat Last Dish[/h1]
description=Remembers the ingredients you put into your last dish, in order, and makes it again from a right-click on an empty bowl or pan. Or lets it cook a dish from the food that goes off first.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Make a salad, stir fry, soup... the usual way. The base item and every ingredient, in order, are recorded.
description=[*]Right-click an empty base of the same kind: [b]Repeat last dish[/b], at the top of the vanilla "Make Salad" (or stir fry, ...) submenu. Select several to make several dishes in a row.
description=[*]Ingredients come from your inventory and the containers next to you (fridge, counters, floor). Fresh or stale doesn't matter, older first. [b]Rotten food is never used.[/b]
description=[*]Normal add-ingredient actions and times; leftovers go back where they came from.
description=[*]Greyed out with a list of what's missing when an ingredient isn't there.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Cook what spoils first[/h2] (new in 0.1.3)
description=Right below it: [b]Cook what spoils first: Salad[/b]. You pick the dish, the mod picks the food that goes off soonest. The tooltip lists what will go in.
description=[list]
description=[*]Only adds that don't make the dish worse, using the game's own rule: a new ingredient makes it nicer, a 2nd of the same adds nothing, a 3rd makes it worse, so it never puts in a 3rd. At Cooking 0 the game stops rewarding after the 4th ingredient, so it stops there.
description=[*]Stale food goes in without a penalty (the dish's freshness comes from the bowl). Then spices: all that go off, and the 2 you have most of among those that keep.
description=[*]Never used: rotten, burnt, favourites, what you hold, food you know is poisoned, raw food a dish can't cook, and wild mushrooms and berries (any of them may be poisoned).
description=[*]The dish becomes your last dish, so you can repeat it.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=Client-side Lua using vanilla actions only. Checked in single player and multiplayer (dedicated server, 0.1.2). "Cook what spoils first" is checked in single player only. Safe to add to or remove from a save.
description=
description=[h2]한국어[/h2]
description=마지막으로 만든 요리(샐러드, 볶음 등)의 재료를 넣은 순서대로 기억했다가, 같은 빈 그릇·프라이팬을 우클릭하면 바닐라 "샐러드 만들기"(볶음 등) 하위 메뉴 맨 위의 [b]마지막 요리 반복[/b]으로 다시 만듭니다. 여러 개를 선택하면 차례로 만듭니다. 재료는 인벤토리와 주변 용기(냉장고 등)에서 꺼내며, 신선도는 상관없이(오래된 것부터) 쓰고 상한 음식은 쓰지 않습니다. 같은 하위 메뉴의 [b]상할 재료부터 요리[/b]는 요리 종류만 고르면 먼저 상할 재료부터 골라 만듭니다. 게임 규칙상 요리 효과가 떨어지는 재료(같은 재료 3번째, 요리 0레벨의 5번째 재료)는 넣지 않고, 상한 것·즐겨찾기·독이 있을 수 있는 야생 버섯과 베리는 쓰지 않습니다. 싱글과 멀티(전용 서버, 0.1.2) 모두 사용 확인했습니다(상할 재료부터 요리는 싱글만 확인).
